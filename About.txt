AI SupportHub is an AI-powered, anonymous harassment and abuse reporting platform designed to create safer environments in schools, workplaces, and community organizations. Many victims of harassment do not report incidents due to fear of retaliation, lack of trust in authorities, or power imbalances. AI SupportHub addresses this gap by providing a secure, confidential channel for reporting while using artificial intelligence to support fair, timely, and data-driven case handling.

The primary target audience includes educational institutions, corporate organizations, NGOs, and community groups seeking reliable systems to manage harassment and abuse cases. Secondary users are individuals—students, employees, or community members—who need a safe and anonymous way to report incidents without risking exposure. The platform is especially impactful in environments where inequality, gender-based harassment, or systemic bias prevents open reporting.

The system operates through a structured and transparent workflow. Reporters submit incidents anonymously via a mobile application, first selecting their organization, then providing written descriptions and optional supporting evidence such as images or audio. Each report is securely stored and passed through an AI processing pipeline. Natural Language Processing models analyze the text to classify the type of abuse and assign a severity score based on threat indicators, repetition, and contextual language. Pattern detection algorithms then compare reports to identify recurring offenders, locations, or behavioral trends.

Administrators access a secure dashboard where reports are automatically prioritized using AI-generated insights, while maintaining human oversight for all decisions. Explainable AI features ensure transparency by showing why reports are flagged or ranked as high priority. The system emphasizes ethical AI usage by avoiding automated punishments and ensuring that all actions involve human review.

AI SupportHub combines anonymity, intelligence, and accountability to reduce silence around harassment, promote equality, and support safer, more inclusive communities.
